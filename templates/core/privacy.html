Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LunaFish handles information when students, parents or guardians, teachers, and other users access our online classroom services.

Effective date September 22, 2026 Applies to LunaFish app and related online services

1. Overview

LunaFish provides an online classroom designed to support communication and learning between students and teachers. We aim to collect and use only the information needed to operate, protect, improve, and support the service.

By using LunaFish, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If a student uses LunaFish through a parent, guardian, school, or teacher-managed arrangement, that responsible adult or organization may also have privacy responsibilities that apply to the student.

2. Information we handle

Depending on how LunaFish is used, we may handle the following categories of information:

  • Account information: information such as a name, username, contact details, account role, profile information, and authentication-related data.
  • Student and teacher information: information needed to connect a student with a teacher, organize classes, manage enrollment, or provide educational services.
  • Classroom activity: class participation, schedules, attendance-related information, exercises, learning activity, progress, results, ratings, or similar educational records created through the service.
  • Communications: messages, support requests, and other content users choose to send through LunaFish.
  • Audio and video data: if a live class uses microphone, camera, or real-time communication features, the service may process audio, video, and connection data necessary to provide that class experience. We do not state that live sessions are recorded unless a recording feature is specifically presented to users.
  • Technical information: IP address, device or browser information, app version, language, timestamps, diagnostic information, and security-related logs.
  • Transaction information: course, package, enrollment, price, payment status, transaction reference, and related billing records. Payment-card or bank credentials may be handled directly by the relevant payment provider rather than LunaFish.

3. How information is used

We may use information to:

  • create and maintain user accounts;
  • connect students and teachers and provide online classroom features;
  • schedule, manage, and support classes and learning activities;
  • deliver communications and user-requested support;
  • process or confirm purchases, enrollments, and payment status;
  • maintain service reliability, prevent abuse, investigate technical issues, and protect users;
  • understand how the service is used and improve LunaFish; and
  • comply with legal, accounting, security, or regulatory obligations where applicable.

4. Classroom communications

LunaFish is built to enable interaction between students and teachers. Information shared inside a classroom may therefore be visible to the teacher, participating student, and other authorized participants or administrators when necessary for the classroom or service to function.

Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive personal information during classes or messages. Teachers and administrators should also use student information only for appropriate educational and support purposes.

5. Children and student privacy

Some LunaFish users may be children or minors. Where consent from a parent, guardian, school, or other authorized adult is required by applicable law, the responsible adult or organization should provide or arrange that consent before the child uses the service.

We encourage parents and guardians to stay involved in a child's use of LunaFish. A parent or guardian may contact us to ask about a child's personal information, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. We may need to verify the requester's authority before acting on such a request.

For young users: do not share passwords, home addresses, financial details, or other private information in classroom messages or live sessions.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only as needed for the service, including with:

  • teachers, students, and authorized account participants when necessary for classroom interaction;
  • service providers that support hosting, real-time communications, infrastructure, messaging, analytics, security, customer support, or other operational functions;
  • payment providers when a purchase or payment must be initiated or verified;
  • professional advisers or authorities when reasonably necessary to meet legal obligations, protect rights and safety, or respond to lawful requests; and
  • a successor organization in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or transfer of the service, subject to appropriate protections.

7. Payments

When a user purchases a course, package, or other paid service, LunaFish may send transaction details to a third-party payment provider and receive a transaction or verification reference in return. The payment provider's own privacy terms apply to information it collects directly from the user.

8. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining educational or transaction records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, protecting users, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods can differ depending on the type of information and the reason it is needed. When information is no longer required, we may delete it, anonymize it, or securely isolate it until deletion is possible.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their passwords, devices, and account access.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain personal information. You may also have the right to object to certain processing or request a portable copy of eligible information.

To make a privacy request, use the contact or support channel available through LunaFish. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity, account, or authority to act for another person. Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, educational-record, or transaction purposes.

11. International processing

Depending on the infrastructure and service providers used to operate LunaFish, information may be processed in countries other than the country where a user is located. Where required, we take appropriate steps to protect information when it is transferred across borders.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as LunaFish changes or as legal and operational requirements evolve. When we make a material update, we may provide notice through the app, website, or another appropriate channel. The effective date at the top of this page shows when this version applies.

13. Contact us

If you have a question, concern, or request about privacy or personal information, please contact LunaFish through the support or contact channel available in the app or on the LunaFish website.
